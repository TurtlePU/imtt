\title{Type Systems That Reinterpret Themselves}

\begin{abstract}
We advocate that type systems should be able to
reinterpret their own terms in any internal model of
themselves: equipped with a single tool of
reinterpretation, you can express a variety of features,
such as universe polymorphism, external parametricity,
ability to do synthetic mathematics internally and more.
To this end, we explore limits of self-reinterpretation
and present two type systems capable of it, namely HM-R
and MLTT-R, prove their basic properties and show
several examples of useful internal models.
\end{abstract}
