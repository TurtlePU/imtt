\begin{abstract}
    We search for type systems capable of ``eating
    themselves'' in the sense that their own metatheoretical
    properties could be stated and proven inside them. In
    this particular instance, we study the consequences of
    introducing a reinterpretation operator $m\llbracket
    t\rrbracket$ which allows instantiation of a closed
    term $t$ as a construction inside a model $m$
    definable nominally in the system. Our main result
    is a design of a two-level type theory with
    reinterpretation, $\twoLTT$, where reinterpretation
    can be used to provide a limited form of universe
    polymorphism, prove external parametricity and do
    some synthetic mathematics.
\end{abstract}
